Dynamic Coherence Model

The MCD is the proprietary operational framework that governs the design of every ecosystem I build — whether a cycling laboratory with DOI publications, an international veterinary clinic with trilingual SEO strategy, or a client’s digital architecture.

01 Precise technical identity. What you are must be verifiable by external systems: DOIs, ORCID, Wikidata, Schema.org @graph. Not descriptions — data.
02 Documented criterion. Every decision has a record. No improvisation goes without an auditable trace. The absence of documentation equals the absence of criterion.
03 Zero tolerance for unpositioned work. If it exists but cannot be found and verified by the systems that make relevance decisions, it is not fulfilling its function.

The MCD operates as the Ravello Law: authority, documentation and structural visibility are the three axes that determine whether an entity exists for Google, for LLMs and for the right people.
